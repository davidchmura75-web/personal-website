About me

I am a student, engineer, and developer committed to learning. Currently, I am a freshman at Northeastern with a combined major in Mechanical Engineering and Computer Science.

I aspire to have my work be fun for me in the future, whether that be designing and programming robots, developing games, or using the skills I learn to create solutions to meaningful problems.

Some of my more unique talents include juggling, beating Minecraft in under 20 minutes, and being able to identify all 195 United Nations countries on a map.

Right now, I am working on designing and creating a remote-controlled robotic dog.
