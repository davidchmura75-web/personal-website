About me

I am a student, engineer, and developer committed to learning. Currently, I am studying at Northeastern with a combined major in Mechanical Engineering and Computer Science.

I aspire to have my work be fun for me in the future, whether that be designing and programming robots, developing games, or using the skills I learn to create solutions to meaningful problems.

Right now, I am working on designing and creating a remote-controlled robotic dog.
