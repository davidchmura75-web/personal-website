For the bill of materials, I tried to go for simple parts, such as using one chip to manage all of the servos, choosing an ESP32 as the microcontroller since I was already familiar with it, and electing to 3d print the majority of the dog for simplicity. Additionally, I chose to use the 2200 mAh battery as that should last long enough for at least 10 minutes of operation while being cheap. Overall, this list is decently expensive for my current financial standing, so I will be sure to test each subsystem before buying materials that could potentially not work well.
